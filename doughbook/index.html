Questions

Before you download

How does Doughbook calculate recipe cost?

You enter ingredients the way you buy them — a 5 lb bag, a dozen eggs — and build recipes in cups or grams. Doughbook converts and works out the cost of one piece, with labor and packaging included.

Can it suggest what to charge?

Yes. It suggests a price that covers your costs and warns you when you are about to sell below your floor.

Does it handle orders and invoices?

Yes. Orders keep the customer, items, date, deposit and balance, and you can send quotes, invoices and receipts as PDF.

Can I print ingredient labels?

Yes. Labels list ingredients in the right order with allergens in bold.

Do I need an account?

No. Your recipes, orders and customers live on your phone, and you can export a full backup whenever you like.

Which devices and languages does Doughbook support?

iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android. The app is available in 10 languages: English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish.
