MoodeeMood tracker and journal

How are you, really? Ten seconds to check in.

Moodee is a calm, private mood tracker and mood journal. Pick one of five moods and, if you like, add a note, a photo, a voice memo, tags, your stress and energy levels, or last night's sleep. Over time your check-ins become a picture of how you are really doing.

Questions

Before you download

How long does a check-in take?

Under 10 seconds. Picking one of five moods is the only required tap; everything else is optional.

Where is my mood data stored?

On your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no sync; your moods, notes, photos and voice memos never leave it.

What insights does Moodee show?

Mood by weekday and by time of day, mood against sleep, stress and energy, and which tags lift you up or drag you down, plus a weekly report and a monthly letter.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Moodee has an app lock.

Can I export my journal?

Yes. PDF export and a full backup are built in.

Which devices and languages does Moodee support?

iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android. The app is available in 12 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Vietnamese.
