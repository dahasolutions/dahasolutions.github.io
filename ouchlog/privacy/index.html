OuchLog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

OuchLog is a pain and symptom diary that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

None of your health data. OuchLog has no account system and no server of its own. Your pain entries, body regions, notes and medications never leave your device, and we (the developer) never receive, see, or store them.

The App Store and Google Play versions use two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage counts: which screens are opened, that an entry was logged, that a report was shared, and purchases. No pain level, body region, note, medication or entry date is ever sent.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (device model, OS version, where in the code the crash happened) so crashes can be fixed.

Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy terms. OuchLog has no advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps.

What the app stores on your device

The information you choose to log:

  • Pain entries: time, pain level, body regions, pain type, triggers, impact on your day, notes
  • Medications, the doses you record and any dose limits you set
  • Doctor visits: date, doctor or clinic, and your questions

This data is stored in a local database inside the app's private storage on your device. It is protected by your device's own security (passcode/Face ID/encryption at rest). You can additionally require Face ID to open the app (More → Privacy).

What leaves your device

Only files you create and share:

  • PDF doctor reports
  • CSV exports
  • JSON backup files

These are generated locally and only leave the device when you explicitly share or save them via the iOS share sheet. Where you send them (Mail, Files, AirDrop, a cloud drive) is your choice and is governed by those services' policies. OuchLog does not store health data in iCloud.

Notifications

Reminders you switch on (daily reminder, check-in after a dose, monthly summary, the evening before a doctor visit) are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server sends them.

Data retention & deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries and medications, or use More → Backup & restore → Delete all data to erase everything inside the app. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Health data

Connecting a health app is optional and off by default. OuchLog asks for permission first, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in the system settings.

  • Apple Health (iPhone): OuchLog saves the pain entries you log as symptoms in Apple Health, and reads your sleep to compare it with your pain on the Trends screen.
  • Health Connect (Android): OuchLog only reads your sleep, for the same comparison. It writes nothing to Health Connect.

Health data read this way stays on your device. It is never sent to Firebase or anywhere else, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud.

Children

OuchLog is not directed at children under 13.
