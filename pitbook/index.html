What it does

Built for whatever you maintain

01

Cars, motorcycles, campers, boats

Each with its own service checklist. Boats and generators can run on engine hours instead of an odometer.

02

Petrol, diesel or electric

An EV logs kWh and skips the oil change entirely.

03

Reminders that respect you

One tap sets up the usual schedule for your kind of vehicle. One daily summary of what is coming due, never a stream of alerts.

04

Fuel economy done properly

Measured between full fill-ups, with partial fills handled. L/100 km, km/L, mpg (US) or mpg (UK) — miles with litres is fine.

05

Where the money goes

Monthly spending, cost by category, cost per mile, and how the price you pay for fuel has moved.

06

Receipts and warranty

Attach receipt photos to any record. Track warranty limits by date or odometer, whichever runs out first.

Questions

Before you download

What vehicles can I track in Pitbook?

Cars, motorcycles, campers and boats, each with its own service checklist. Petrol, diesel and electric are supported, and boats or generators can use engine hours.

How do service reminders work?

Pitbook tracks what is due by date, by odometer, or both, counting from the last time you actually did the work, and sends one daily summary of what is coming due.

Does it calculate fuel economy?

Yes, between full fill-ups, with partial fills handled properly, in L/100 km, km/L, mpg (US) or mpg (UK).

Can I import my existing log?

Yes. Import a CSV from your spreadsheet. You can also export your records as CSV or JSON and back up everything to a single file.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and Pitbook works entirely offline.

Which devices and languages does Pitbook support?

iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android. The app is available in 20 languages: English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese.
