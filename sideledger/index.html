Questions

Before you download

Who is Side Ledger for?

Anyone in the US with 1099 income and nobody withholding taxes for them: rideshare and delivery drivers, online sellers, creators and freelancers.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank login and no account. You log payouts, expenses and mileage yourself, in about three taps each.

How is the tax estimate calculated?

From what you log, using built-in federal rates and state taxes, with a step-by-step breakdown of self-employment tax, income tax and the QBI deduction.

Does it track quarterly estimated taxes?

Yes. It shows a countdown to every IRS quarterly deadline, reminds you before each one, and lets you mark payments as paid.

Does Side Ledger file my taxes?

No. It estimates what you owe and exports a CSV and a one-page PDF summary for you or your accountant.

Which devices and languages does Side Ledger support?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) and Android. The app is in English.
